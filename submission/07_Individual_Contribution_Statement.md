# Individual Contribution Statement

**Project:** RevenuePulse AI - Sales Opportunity & Customer Revenue-Decline Prediction
Copilot
**Course:** MBA ZG583 capstone
**Student:** Suhani Kanwar

This capstone was completed in **individual mode** according to the submitted Topic
Approval Form. No team members contributed to this submission, and no peer validation
of the product, model or commercial case was performed or is claimed.

## Scope of individual responsibility

The student is responsible for:

- **Product strategy** - problem selection, target users, wedge, positioning as
  decision support only, and the recommendation to iterate rather than scale.
- **Requirements** - PRD, functional and non-functional requirements, acceptance
  criteria, MVP scope and feature prioritisation.
- **UX decisions** - the four-page review workflow (dashboard, queue, detail, human
  review), risk-band presentation, evidence placement and failure states.
- **Data and model design** - synthetic data contract, point-in-time snapshots,
  feature allow-list, revenue-decline label definition, time-ordered splits, baseline
  scorecard, model selection rule, evaluation framework and explainability approach.
- **Prototype** - the Streamlit application, product service layer, rule-based
  recommendation logic and SQLite review audit log.
- **Business model** - commercial phases, pilot design and terms, pricing hypotheses,
  and the decision not to calculate LTV/CAC without evidence.
- **Risk and governance** - ethics note, stop conditions, data-handling terms,
  prohibited uses and launch blockers.
- **Final review** - verification of every reported metric against the reproducible
  evaluation output, and the decision to label all evidence as synthetic.

## AI disclosure

AI tools assisted with planning, drafting, coding and review. Specifically, AI coding
agents supported repository scaffolding, the synthetic data generator, snapshot and
feature code, model training and evaluation scripts, the Streamlit prototype, tests and
documentation drafts.

Final judgment, verification and submission responsibility remained with the student.
That includes the choice of problem, the label definition, the decision to keep the
transparent baseline, the rejection of class weighting, the interpretation of the
synthetic results, the "iterate, do not scale" recommendation, and the confirmation
that no fabricated metrics, user research or pilot evidence appears in the submission.

## What is not claimed

- No peer review, team contribution or external validation occurred.
- No real customer data, customer quotes, interviews, pilot results or revenue outcomes
  are included.
- No real-world model performance, product-market fit or willingness to pay is claimed.
